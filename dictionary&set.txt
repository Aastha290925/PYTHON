14.Take user input for roll no of 5 students and their coma separated marks of 6 subjects (out of 50). Display 	the minimum and maximum marks of each subject in a separate dictionary object.

all_student_marks = []

for i in range(5):
    roll = input(f"Enter Roll No for student {i+1}: ")
    marks_input = input("Enter comma-separated marks for 6 subjects: ")
    # Convert input string to list of integers
    marks_list = [int(m.strip()) for m in marks_input.split(",")]
    all_student_marks.append(marks_list)

# Initializing min and max tracking for 6 subjects
min_marks = [999] * 6
max_marks = [-1] * 6

for marks in all_student_marks:
    for subj_idx in range(6):
        if marks[subj_idx] < min_marks[subj_idx]:

        if marks[subj_idx] > max_marks[subj_idx]:
            max_marks[subj_idx] = marks[subj_idx]

summary_dict = {

15.Create a dictionary of 6 fruits (by taking user input) and store their buying and selling price as a tuple 	value. Display the fruit name whose buying price is less than its selling price and also the fruit name 	whose buying price is more than its selling price. Display the fruit that has earned no profit no loss.

fruit_store = {}

for i in range(6):
    fruit = input(f"Enter name of fruit {i+1}: ").lower()
    bp = float(input("Enter Buying Price (CP): "))
    sp = float(input("Enter Selling Price (SP): "))
    fruit_store[fruit] = (bp, sp)
    print("-" * 20)

profit_fruits = []
loss_fruits = []
no_profit_loss = []

for fruit, prices in fruit_store.items():
    bp, sp = prices
    if bp < sp:
        profit_fruits.append(fruit)
    elif bp > sp:
        loss_fruits.append(fruit)
    else:
        no_profit_loss.append(fruit)

print("Fruits with Profit (Buying < Selling):", profit_fruits)
print("Fruits with Loss (Buying > Selling):", loss_fruits)
print("Fruits with Break-even (No Profit No Loss):", no_profit_loss)

output:
Enter name of fruit 1: Apple
Enter Buying Price (CP): 80
Enter Selling Price (SP): 100
